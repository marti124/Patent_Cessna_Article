Beyond the Skyhawk

The airplane is one case of a wider pattern. The rule that let one company keep building to an older certification basis, while everyone else was held to a newer one, has a counterpart in nearly every regulated market. The same post-1970 wall keeps eighteen other groups of Americans from building what their own expired patents already released to the public — farmers, truckers, hospitals, laundries, apartment owners, school districts, volunteer fire departments.
